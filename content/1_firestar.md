Rusty is a kittypet who is very interested by the forest outside his Towlegplace. After being invited to join ThunderClan to learn the ways of the wild, he receives the name Firepaw and befriends Graypaw and his fellow apprentices. Firepaw is assigned to Lionheart and Tigerclaw for training, but later Bluestar takes him as her own apprentice. He makes a friendship with Yellowfang. after finding her in the forest, and is happy to see her become ThunderClan's medicine cat after Spottedleaf's (the previous medicine cat’s) death. However, after Tigerclaw is named deputy after Lionheart's death, and Ravenpaw (Tigerclaw’s apprentice) reveals that Tigerclaw murdered Redtail and that he didn’t die in a accident in hopes of becoming deputy, and Firepaw and Graypaw guide Ravenpaw to live at Barley's farm for his own protection. After driving the cruel, and treacherous Brokenstar out of ShadowClan, he is given the warrior name Fireheart. Along with his best friend Graystripe, Fireheart guides WindClan from there exile, back home, and helps RiverClan when their territory floods.

Fireheart is given Cinderpaw as an apprentice, but the young, gray, she-cat gets injured on the Thunderpath in one of Tigerclaw's schemes. He is appointed to finish Brackenpaw's training because Graystripe, his friend is off seeing Silverstream, who had saved him when he fell into a river. Fireheart is given his nephew Cloudpaw as an apprentice, and struggles to rein his ignorance towards the warrior code. Tigerclaw's treachery is revealed to the Clan during a failed attack on Bluestar's life, and Fireheart is made deputy in wake of his banishment. He attempts to hold a fragile ThunderClan together as Bluestar's judgement and faith in StarClan disappears, and ThunderClan seeks help from RiverClan when a fire ravages their camp. Fireheart also mentors Tigerclaw's son Bramblepaw, but struggles not to see his father's shadow. Upon learning that Tigerstar, now leader of ShadowClan, plans to lure a pack of viscous dogs to ThunderClan's camp, Fireheart rallies on his Clanmates to lead the dogs away from the camp. This plan unfortunately failed, but was fixed by Bluestar sacrificing her life during saving Firehearts.
Upon Bluestar's death, Fireheart ascends to leader as Firestar and gains his nine lives, but is warned of blood ruling the forest. He appoints Whitestorm as his deputy, and together they rebuild the Clan. Tigerstar joins ShadowClan and RiverClan to form TigerClan and brings BloodClan, a cruel gang of rouges, to the forest, in hopes of destroying ThunderClan and WindClan. However, their leader Scourge kills Tigerstar and threatens to take control of the whole forest for himself. Firestar forms the four Clans into LionClan, and together they are able to drive BloodClan from the forest. Whitestorm dies during the battle, and Firestar appoints Graystripe as ThunderClan's new deputy. And peace is newly brought to the clans finally.

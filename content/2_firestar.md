Firestar and his mate Sandstorm have had two daughters, Squirrelpaw and Leafpaw. He becomes concerned when Cinderpelt reveals a prophecy concerning the kin of fire and tiger, along with danger. However, his attempts to keep Squirrelpaw and Brambleclaw apart because of it simply spur them further into leaving on a journey. Firestar, not knowing where they are, is devastated by the loss. Twoleg monsters begin to destroy the forest,to build a new thunderpath, and the leader struggles to keep his Clan well-fed, beacuse the loud towleg monsters scare away all the prey.
Brambleclaw and Squirrelpaw return from their journey and bring the news that StarClan has a plan for them to move to a brand new peacefull home. While the other Clans are resistant, Firestar agrees they should go - when all four Clans travel together. After the destruction of ShadowClan's camp, the four Clans start the Great Journey to the new territory. The Tribe of Rushing Water (basicly a mountin clan) assists them over the mountains, and they eventually arrive at the lake territories (the new territory). Firestar helps ThunderClan settle into their new home, and supports Onewhisker's leadership during Mudclaw's rebellion.
Badgers attack ThunderClan's camp and heavily damage it, and the leader welcomes Stormfur and Brook when they wish to stay and help ThunderClan. ShadowClan tries to take advantage of ThunderClan's weakness, and try to take over the territory, but are beaten off. Firestar accepts the loss of Graystripe and appoints Brambleclaw as deputy. Brambleclaw struggles with the thought that he is offered the chance to kill Firestar and take leadership for himself, but when Hawkwing tries to kill Firestar he instantly regreated all these thoghts and saves his leader from losing all his lives.
